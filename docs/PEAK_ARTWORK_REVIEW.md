# Mountain artwork review

Reviewed 4 October 2026. All 50 featured mountains have an individual composition and a photographic reference. The application keeps its illustrated paper-and-green style. References guide landform and composition; the drawings are simplified interpretations, not measured panoramas or pixel-accurate photo traces.

## Selection rules

Choose a clear view of the mountain itself, with its shoulders, summit and characteristic surrounding terrain visible. Avoid summit selfies, cloud-obscured tops, aerial-only views, photos looking away from the mountain, and neighbouring peaks incorrectly returned by search. Prefer the approach side when it shows the landform well; a recognisable mountain portrait may use a different side. Preserve flat caps, long escarpments, broad corries and the scale of lower hills. Keep the full composition in square thumbnails.

## Regeneration and review

Run `node scripts/render-peak-artwork.cjs`. Keep reference URLs and composition notes with each change. The generator requires complete coverage and does not use the historical SRTM profiles. All SVGs were rasterised successfully and inspected in catalogue contact sheets; thumbnail fitting uses `object-contain`. Existing route links and database image paths are unchanged.
